About

Based in Ibadan, Nigeria. Over 5 years creating visual identity and running ongoing content.

I'm a graphic designer and social media manager with experience designing flyers, brand identities, logos, motion graphics and social content for schools, small businesses and independent brands.

Design is where I started, but it's not where I stop. I manage social pages on an ongoing basis — planning content calendars, keeping brand voice consistent, and thinking about what happens after something goes live.

I'm a fast thinker who adapts quickly to new tools and shifting briefs, comfortable using AI tools to speed up research and first drafts, and highly proficient in Canva, Microsoft Office and Google Workspace.
